Lesson 2.1a: Describing Motion (描述運動)

How far? Which way? How fast? In this lesson you learn to describe motion with words, numbers, arrows and graphs.

displacement (位移)Straight line from start to end, with direction.
scalar (標量) / vector (矢量)Size only / size and direction.
average (平均) / instantaneous (瞬時)Over a whole time / at one moment.

Idea 1

Distance (路程) and Displacement (位移)

Distance (路程)The total length of the path travelled. No direction. Unit: m.
Displacement (位移)The straight-line distance from start to end, with a direction. Unit: m.
🏫 Walking to schoolYou walk 400 m east, then 300 m north.
Distance = 400 + 300 = 700 m.
The path and the displacement make a right-angled triangle.
✏️ Find the displacement with Pythagoras' theorem (畢氏定理)
  1. Formula: (displacement)² = (east part)² + (north part)²
  2. Substitute: s² = 400² + 300² = 160 000 + 90 000 = 250 000
  3. Calculate: s = √250 000 = 500 m
  4. Direction: tan θ = 300 ÷ 400 = 0.75, so θ = tan⁻¹(0.75) ≈ 37° north of east.
Answer: displacement = 500 m, 37° north of east (always give size and direction).
On a straight lineChoose one direction as + (e.g. east or right), so the other is −.
Walk 5 m east then 3 m west: distance = 8 m, displacement = (+5) + (−3) = +2 m (2 m east).
Idea 2

Scalars (標量) and Vectors (矢量)

Scalar (標量): size onlydistance, speed, time, mass, energy
Vector (矢量): size + directiondisplacement, velocity, acceleration, force

How to represent a vector: draw an arrow ➜

  • The length of the arrow shows the size (use a scale).
  • The arrow points in the direction.
  • On a straight line, we can also use a sign: +20 m/s (right), −20 m/s (left).
ExampleScale: 1 square = 10 m.
Displacement 40 m east → an arrow 4 squares long, pointing east.

You will draw your own vector arrows in the next practice. ✍️

Idea 3

Speed (速率) and Velocity (速度): average and instantaneous

Average speed (平均速率)= distance travelled ÷ time
Average velocity (平均速度)= displacement ÷ time

Unit: m s⁻¹ (m/s). Speed is a scalar. Velocity is a vector.

A speedometer shows the instantaneous speed.
Instantaneous speed / velocity (瞬時速率 / 瞬時速度) The speed (or velocity) at one moment. Average speed is for the whole trip.
🚆 MTR train between two stations The train travels 1200 m in 80 s. Average speed = 1200 ÷ 80 = 15 m s⁻¹.
But at the stations its instantaneous speed is 0, and in the middle it may reach about 22 m s⁻¹. The average hides these changes.
🏃 One lap of a 400 m track in 100 s Average speed = 400 ÷ 100 = 4 m s⁻¹. Displacement = 0, so average velocity = 0.
Describing motionSame speed can mean different velocity: 20 m/s east and 20 m/s west have the same speed but different velocities.

Idea 4

Uniform motion (勻速運動): s = vt

Uniform motion means the velocity is constant: the same speed, in the same direction, all the time.

Algebraic method (代數方法) s = v t   (displacement = velocity × time)
Also: v = s ÷ t and t = s ÷ v
✏️ Example: MTR at constant velocityAn MTR train moves at a constant 20 m s⁻¹ for 30 s. How far does it go?
  1. Formula: s = vt
  2. Substitute: s = 20 × 30
  3. Calculate: s = 600 m

Graphical method (圖像方法)

s–t graph: a straight sloping line. Gradient = 600 ÷ 30 = 20 m s⁻¹ = velocity.

v–t graph: a horizontal line. Area = 20 × 30 = 600 m = displacement.

Idea 5

Three motion graphs: s–t, v–t and a–t

Look along each row. Each row is one kind of motion. (Right / forward = +.)

Motions–t (位移-時間)v–t (速度-時間)a–t (加速度-時間)
At rest (靜止)
Uniform velocity, forward
Speeding up (constant a)
Slowing down (constant a) to a stop
Uniform velocity, backward
Golden rules ⭐
  • Gradient (slope, 斜率) of the s–t graph = velocity.
  • Area under the v–t graph = displacement.
  • Gradient of the v–t graph = acceleration (more in Lesson 2.1b).
  • For uniform motion, the a–t graph is on the a = 0 line.
Idea 6

Reading graphs with numbers: gradient and area

✏️ Gradient of an s–t graph
  1. Pick two points on the line: (0 s, 0 m) and (10 s, 50 m).
  2. Gradient = rise ÷ run = Δs ÷ Δt = (50 − 0) ÷ (10 − 0)
  3. = 5 m s⁻¹ = the velocity.
✏️ Area under a v–t graph
  1. Split the shape: a triangle (0–5 s) and a rectangle (5–10 s).
  2. Triangle = ½ × base × height = ½ × 5 × 10 = 25 m
  3. Rectangle = 5 × 10 = 50 m
  4. Displacement = 25 + 50 = 75 m
Tips
  • Read the units on each axis first.
  • If the line does not start at 0, use Δs = (end value − start value).
  • Area below the time axis means moving backwards (negative displacement).

Idea 7

Measuring motion: ticker tape (紙帶) and data logger (數據記錄器)

Ticker-tape timer (打點計時器) The timer makes 50 dots every second (50 Hz) on a paper tape pulled by a trolley.
Time between two dots (1 tick) = 1 ÷ 50 = 0.02 s. So 5 ticks = 0.1 s, 10 ticks = 0.2 s.
✏️ Velocity from a tape A 5-tick section (5 spaces) is 3.0 cm long.
  1. Time = 5 × 0.02 = 0.1 s; length = 3.0 cm = 0.030 m
  2. Formula: average velocity = displacement ÷ time
  3. Substitute: v = 0.030 ÷ 0.1
  4. Calculate: v = 0.30 m s⁻¹

Data logger with a motion sensor (運動感應器) or light gate (光閘)

A motion sensor measures the position many times each second. The data logger records a table and draws graphs. We can turn displacement–time data into velocity:

t / s00.51.01.52.0

Δs in each 0.5 s / m—0.250.751.251.75

The velocity goes up by the same amount each 0.5 s, so the trolley is speeding up uniformly.

Light gateA card 5.0 cm long blocks the light beam for 0.025 s.
Velocity = 0.050 m ÷ 0.025 s = 2.0 m s⁻¹ (almost the instantaneous velocity, because the time is very short).
