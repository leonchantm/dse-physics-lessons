Lesson 2.5a: Momentum & Impulse (動量和衝量)

Why is a slow truck harder to stop than a fast football? Why do airbags save lives? The answers use momentum.

Key wordShort meaning
momentum (動量), pQuantity of motion: p = mv. Unit kg m s⁻¹. A vector.
impulse (衝量)Net force × time = change in momentum. Unit N s.
impact force (撞擊力)The (average) force during a collision.
time of impact (撞擊時間)The time over which the momentum changes (time of contact).

Idea 1

Momentum: the quantity of motion

A moving object that is heavy or fast is hard to stop. Momentum measures this “amount of motion”.

Definition ⭐p = mv
p: momentum (kg m s⁻¹), m: mass (kg), v: velocity (m s⁻¹)
Momentum is a vector: same direction as the velocity.
Remember the signChoose a positive direction. A 2 kg ball moving left at 3 m s⁻¹ has p = −6 kg m s⁻¹ if right is +.
Objectmvp = mv
🚚 truck (slow)10 000 kg2 m s⁻¹20 000 kg m s⁻¹
🚗 car1500 kg20 m s⁻¹30 000 kg m s⁻¹
🏃 sprinter70 kg10 m s⁻¹700 kg m s⁻¹
⚽ football0.45 kg25 m s⁻¹11.25 kg m s⁻¹

Idea 2

A net force acting for some time changes the momentum

From Newton’s Second LawFnet = ma = m(v − u)/t  ⇒  Fnet t = mv − mu = Δp
Impulse = Fnet × t = change in momentum. Unit: N s (= kg m s⁻¹). A vector.
✏️ Ball rebounding from a wallA 0.2 kg ball hits a wall at 5 m s⁻¹ and rebounds at 3 m s⁻¹. Take towards the wall as +.
u = +5, v = −3
Δp = mv − mu = 0.2(−3) − 0.2(5) = −1.6 kg m s⁻¹
(1.6 kg m s⁻¹ away from the wall)
Common mistake ⚠️Writing Δp = 0.2 × (5 − 3) = 0.4. When an object rebounds, the velocity changes sign, so the speeds add: Δp = m(5 + 3).
🧪 Measuring the change in momentumLet a trolley run through a light gate (or past a motion sensor), bounce off a spring buffer and come back through the gate. The data logger gives u and v, so Δp = mv − mu can be found.
Idea 3

Force as the rate of change of momentum

Newton’s Second Law (momentum form) ⭐The net force acting on an object is equal to the rate of change of momentum of the object:
Fnet = (mv − mu) ÷ t = Δp ÷ Δt

For the same change in momentum: a longer time of impact → a smaller impact force; a shorter time → a larger force.

✏️ Example (take g to be 10 m s⁻²)A 2 kg ball drops from rest from a height of 5 m and rebounds to the same height. The time of impact with the ground is 0.2 s. Find the force on the ball by the ground.
  1. Speed before impact: v = √(2gh) = √(2 × 10 × 5) = 10 m s⁻¹ (down). It leaves at 10 m s⁻¹ (up).
  2. Take up as +: Δp = 2(+10) − 2(−10) = 40 kg m s⁻¹
  3. Net force = Δp ÷ t = 40 ÷ 0.2 = 200 N (up)
  4. Net force = R − mg ⇒ R = 200 + 20 = 220 N
Careful ⚠️Δp ÷ t gives the net force. If weight also acts during the impact, add it to find the force by the ground.
Idea 4

Force–time graph: area = impulse

In a real collision the force is not constant. A force sensor connected to a data logger records how F changes with t.

Key fact ⭐Area under the F–t graph = impulse = change in momentum
average force = area ÷ time of impact
✏️ Tennis serveThe F–t graph of a racket hitting a 0.06 kg ball (at rest) is a triangle: peak 300 N, duration 0.02 s.
Impulse = area = ½ × 0.02 × 300 = 3 N s
v = Δp ÷ m = 3 ÷ 0.06 = 50 m s⁻¹
Average force = 3 ÷ 0.02 = 150 N
Idea 5

Safety designs: make the time of impact longer

In a crash, the change in momentum is fixed (from moving to stopped). The only way to reduce the impact force is to increase the time of impact.

DesignHow it works
🎈 AirbagInflates in a crash; the head sinks into it, so it takes longer to stop → smaller force (also spreads the force over a larger area).
🔒 Seat beltStretches slightly, so the passenger stops over a longer time instead of hitting the dashboard.
🚗 Crumple zoneFront and rear of the car crumple in a collision, lengthening the time to stop.
⛑️ HelmetSoft foam lining lengthens the time for the head to stop.
🤸 Landing mat, bending kneesLonger time to stop the body when landing.
🧤 Catching a ball, boxing glovesMoving the hands back with the ball / spongy gloves increase the time of impact.
How to explain in an exam ✍️“The change in momentum is the same. The ___ increases the time of impact. Since F = Δp ÷ t, the impact force is reduced.”
