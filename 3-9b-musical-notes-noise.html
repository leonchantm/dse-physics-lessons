Lesson 3.9b: Musical Notes and Noise (樂音與噪音)

Why does a violin sound different from a piano playing the same note? How loud is too loud? You will use a CRO to “see” sound and describe notes by pitch, loudness and quality.

Key wordShort meaning
CRO (陰極射線示波器)Shows the electrical signal from a microphone as a graph against time.
pitch (音調)How high or low a note sounds: depends on frequency.
loudness (響度)How loud a note sounds: depends on amplitude.
quality (timbre) (音品)What makes instruments sound different: depends on the waveform.
decibel (dB) (分貝)Unit of sound intensity level.

Idea 1

Displaying sound with a microphone and a CRO

  • A microphone turns the sound into an electrical signal; the CRO plots that signal (vertical) against time (horizontal).
  • The time base sets how much time each horizontal division represents, e.g. 1 ms/div.
  • The trace looks like a transverse wave, but it is a graph of the sound against time; the sound itself is longitudinal.
Reading a CRO trace ⭐period T = (divisions for one cycle) × (time base)
frequency f = 1/T
Taller trace (same settings) ⇒ larger amplitude ⇒ louder.
ExampleTime base 2 ms/div. One complete wave covers 4 divisions.
T = 4 × 2 ms = 8 ms = 0.008 s (1M)
f = 1/0.008 = 125 Hz (1A)
In air (340 m s⁻¹): λ = 340/125 = 2.72 m.
Common mistakes ⚠️Counting the divisions for half a wave; forgetting to convert ms to s; comparing amplitudes of traces taken at different Y-gain settings.

Idea 3

Sound intensity level and the decibel

Decibel scale ⭐
  • Sound intensity level is measured in decibels (dB) with a sound level meter.
  • 0 dB = threshold of hearing: the faintest sound a person can just hear (not “no sound”).
  • Every +10 dB means the intensity is ×10: +20 dB ⇒ ×100, +30 dB ⇒ ×1000.
ExampleA street at 80 dB compared with a conversation at 60 dB: +20 dB ⇒ intensity ×100.
SourceLevel / dBEffect

Idea 4

Noise pollution and acoustic protection

Effects of noiseLong exposure to loud noise causes hearing loss (often permanent), tinnitus, stress, sleep problems and poorer concentration. Hong Kong streets can exceed 80 dB, mainly from traffic and construction.
Reducing noise ⭐
  • At the source: quieter machines, silencers, limiting construction hours and night-time noise.
  • Along the path: noise barriers and enclosures beside highways, double-glazed windows, trees, distance.
  • At the receiver: ear plugs/ear muffs in noisy workplaces; soft furnishings and sound-absorbing panels.
Protect your hearingKeep earphone volume low and limit listening time; take breaks from noisy places; wear ear protectors at work in loud environments.
Being a good neighbour (STSE)Avoid loud music and renovation at night; place speakers away from shared walls; be aware of noise rules in your estate.
Data skillsExam questions may give a relationship, e.g. “intensity I is inversely proportional to the square of the distance d”. To test it, plot I against 1/d²: a straight line through the origin supports the claim.
