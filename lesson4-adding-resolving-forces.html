Lesson 4: Adding and Resolving Forces (力的合成與分解)

Forces are vectors. When two people pull a box in different directions, where does it go? How much of a slanted pull actually moves a suitcase forward?

Key wordShort meaning
coplanar forces (共面力)Forces that all lie in the same flat plane.
resultant / net force (合力 / 淨力)The single force with the same effect as all the forces together (the vector sum).
tip-to-tail method (首尾相接法)Draw the arrows one after another; the resultant goes from the first tail to the last tip.
parallelogram of forces (力的平行四邊形)Draw two forces from one point; the diagonal is the resultant.
resolve, component (分解、分量)Split one force into two perpendicular parts.
equilibrium (平衡)Net force = 0.
You will needPythagoras’ theorem and sin, cos, tan (set your calculator to degrees, “D”).

Idea 1

Adding forces (力的合成): by drawing

Forces along one lineSame direction → add. Opposite directions → subtract (choose one direction as +).
Example: 12 N left and 4 N right → net force = 12 + (−4) = 8 N to the left.

Method 1: tip-to-tail (首尾相接)

  1. Choose a scale, e.g. 1 cm = 1 N.
  2. Draw F₁ as an arrow to scale.
  3. Start F₂ at the tip of F₁.
  4. The resultant goes from the tail of F₁ to the tip of F₂. Measure its length (→ size) and angle (→ direction) with a ruler and protractor.

Method 2: parallelogram (平行四邊形法)

Draw F₁ and F₂ from the same point, complete the parallelogram; the diagonal from that point is the resultant. (Same answer!)

Check your answerThe resultant of two forces is never bigger than F₁ + F₂ (both the same way) and never smaller than |F₁ − F₂| (opposite ways). For 4 N and 3 N: between 1 N and 7 N.
Idea 2

Adding forces by calculation

✏️ Two perpendicular forces6 N east and 8 N north act on a point.
  1. Size (Pythagoras): F = √(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10 N
  2. Direction: tan θ = 8 ÷ 6 → θ = 53.1° north of east
✏️ Any two forces: use componentsF₁ = 10 N at 0° and F₂ = 10 N at 60° (to the x-axis).
  1. x: 10 + 10 cos 60° = 10 + 5 = 15 N
  2. y: 0 + 10 sin 60° = 8.66 N
  3. F = √(15² + 8.66²) = 17.3 N; tan θ = 8.66 ÷ 15 → θ = 30°
Method ⭐ (components)① Resolve every force into x and y parts. ② Add all the x parts; add all the y parts. ③ F = √(Fx² + Fy²), tan θ = Fy ÷ Fx.
Equilibrium (平衡) of three forcesIf an object stays at rest under three forces, their resultant is zero: any one force is equal and opposite to the resultant of the other two. Drawn tip-to-tail, the three forces form a closed triangle.
✏️ A picture on two strings (take g = 10 m s⁻²)A 3 kg picture hangs from two vertical strings with equal tension T.
  1. Up = down: 2T = W = mg = 3 × 10 = 30 N
  2. T = 15 N

Idea 3

Resolving a force (力的分解)

Resolving is adding backwards: we replace one force by two perpendicular components that together have the same effect.

Components of F at angle θ to the horizontal Fx = F cos θ (horizontal)
Fy = F sin θ (vertical)

Graphically: draw F to scale, then draw a rectangle with F as the diagonal. Measure the two sides.

✏️ Pulling a suitcase 🧳You pull a suitcase with 40 N at 30° above the horizontal.
  1. Forward part: F cos 30° = 40 × 0.866 = 34.6 N (this moves it forward)
  2. Upward part: F sin 30° = 40 × 0.5 = 20 N (this lifts it a little, so the floor pushes up less)
cos goes with the side next to θ; sin goes with the side opposite θ.
Check(F cos θ)² + (F sin θ)² = F². Each component is smaller than F.
Idea 4

Forces on an inclined plane (斜面)

For an object on a slope, resolve the forces along the slope and perpendicular to the slope (the object can only move along the slope).

Weight componentsalong the slope (down): W sin θ
perpendicular (into the slope): W cos θ
  • No motion perpendicular to the slope → R = W cos θ.
  • Block at rest on a rough slope → friction balances the slope component: f = W sin θ (up the slope).
  • Smooth slope (no friction) → the net force is W sin θ down the slope.
✏️ Example (g = 9.81 m s⁻²)A 2 kg block rests on a rough slope at 30°.
  1. W = 2 × 9.81 = 19.6 N
  2. Along: W sin 30° = 9.81 N → friction = 9.81 N up the slope
  3. Perpendicular: W cos 30° = 17.0 N → R = 17.0 N
Where does θ go?The angle between W and the perpendicular to the slope equals the slope angle θ. Check with extremes: θ = 0 (flat) → along-slope part W sin 0 = 0 ✓.

Practice: resolving forces

Try it!

Simulation: force vectors

g = 9.81 m s⁻². The block is held at rest by friction.

Things to try
  • Add: F₁ = 4 N at 0°, F₂ = 3 N at 90° → resultant 5 N at 36.9°. Now tick the parallelogram box: the same resultant!
  • Add: make the angle between F₁ and F₂ 0°, then 180°. When is the resultant largest? smallest?
  • Resolve: change θ from 0° to 90°. Watch F cos θ shrink and F sin θ grow.
  • Slope: increase θ. The friction needed (W sin θ) grows; R (W cos θ) shrinks.
